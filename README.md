# Hello, 3D

An interactive page that shows a young child how flat shapes become solid shapes.

- **Flat and solid**: three sliders (wide, tall, deep) turn a flat rectangle into a cuboid or a cube.
- **Spin**: a circle, rectangle or triangle spins around a pole to make a sphere, cylinder or cone.
- **Fold**: flat paper folds or rolls into a cube, cuboid, cylinder, cone, pyramid or prism.
- **Stack**: copies of a flat shape pile up into a cube, cuboid, cylinder, prism, cone, pyramid or sphere.
- **Quiz**: 10 yes/no questions per round, picked at random.

## Use

Download the repository and open `index.html` in a browser. It needs no server and no internet connection; `three.min.js` (three.js r149, MIT licence) sits next to it.
